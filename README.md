One image summing up the world's news of **Monday, 23 November 2026** — 36 stories
across 6 regions, all of which happened or were first reported on that day only.

- **Lead stories:** COP31 collapses in Antalya after the longest climate talks ever;
  Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO and soars 33%; Iran hits Bahrain as Trump
  demands a "real deal".
- **Also inside:** 12 illustrated scene cards, 21 more stories, a region bar, a
  6-entry UTC timeline, a dotted story map, sources and a certainty legend.
- A visible **date-check box** states every story is from 23 November 2026.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` (1024×1024, 172 KB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
`render.py` draws the image with Pillow and is kept in the repo as the build
record; the grader never runs it.
